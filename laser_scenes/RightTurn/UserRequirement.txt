SafeBench right-turn (Xu et al., arXiv:2206.09682, Fig. 4g).

Ego approaches Town10HD_Opt junction 189 from the south and turns RIGHT onto the
eastbound arm. A crossing vehicle on the west→east approach is released when the
ego nears the conflict point (~-48.7, 24.5) and drives through the junction,
forcing collision avoidance.

Road flag: T10J189Right
  lane 1 = ego approach (south of junction)
  lane 2 = crossing west→east traffic

global plan (see laser/target_vehicle/target_vehicle.py).
